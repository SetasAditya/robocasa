LabApt manipulation challenge

Ten task profiles · two overlapping families · three difficulty levels · measured layout 100.

Every profile starts experimental. A scored scene requires verified geometry, cameras, stable reset, complete state replay and a successful witness replay. Policy results and physical validation are separate evidence.

What changes?

Kitchen geometry stays fixed. Task objects, constrained placements and recorded initialization checkpoints vary. Numerical ranges are development defaults and must pass geometry review before formal scene freezing.

Evaluation contract

Prepare 20 practice and 50 formal scenes per task, difficulty and initialization mode. Methods share frozen scenes, observations, action limits and success rules. Record controller development, demonstration and pretraining budgets separately. Report diagnostic runs separately from qualified formal runs.

Tray guides are removable tabletop apparatus. Wiping scores physical contact coverage, not cleaning chemistry. Rack slot bounds and bowl seated-height intervals require asset-specific collision review. No synthetic force sensor or fixture geometry change is introduced.
